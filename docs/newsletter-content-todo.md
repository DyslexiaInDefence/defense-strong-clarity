## Partner / organisation recognition

### Forces Transition Group (FTG) — https://ftgjobfairs.com/
Status: to include in next newsletter.

Why recognise them: FTG helps military and blue light personnel — plus spouses,
siblings and veterans — transition from service life to civilian employment.
Everything they provide to Service Users is free.

What they offer (verified on their site, Aug 2026):
- Online Job Fairs and Live Transition Days (next: Cheltenham Town Hall, 15 September 2026)
- Live jobs board across engineering, logistics, cyber/IT, rail, finance and more
- Free CV writing support
- FTG Academy — course providers including Learning Curve Group, Breeze and Murphy Solution
- "Project You, Project Me", IDENTITY workshop and VALOUR programmes
- The Five Fundamentals to Success: CV Writing, CPD Alignment, Networking, Financials, Elevation
- The "Sleepwalker / Hoper / Hunter" framing on taking ownership of your transition

Dyslexia in Defence angle: transition paperwork, CV writing and interviews are
exactly the points where dyslexic strengths get hidden by process. FTG's free,
practical support pairs well with our guidance on leaving service (JSP 822 vs
Equality Act 2010).

Newsletter copy notes:
- Use the full name "Forces Transition Group" on first mention, then FTG.
- Make clear FTG is a separate independent organisation; this is recognition,
  not a formal partnership or endorsement of Dyslexia in Defence by FTG.
- Link to https://ftgjobfairs.com/ and, if relevant, the Transition Day page.
